Founded by Matteo Comisso, MC Software is a one-person studio focused on small, native apps that respect your time, your device, and your data. No growth loops, no dark patterns, no server-side surveillance dressed up as "insight."

The apps are deliberately small in ambition and careful in execution. Most of them run entirely on-device. The ones that don't — because the world isn't yet ready to live without the cloud — are honest about what leaves your phone and why.
