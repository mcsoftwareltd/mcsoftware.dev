## Summary

MapInk is a local iPad app for annotating maps. It has no MC Software account system, no MC Software backend, no advertising SDK, and no tracking SDK. Your saved map projects are stored on your device.

The app uses Apple's MapKit services to display maps, search for places and addresses, and calculate driving or walking directions. Location access is optional and only used to center the map near you.

"We," "us," and "MC Software" refer to **MC Software ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales under company number **16230837**. Contact: [matteo@mcsoftware.dev](mailto:matteo@mcsoftware.dev).

## 2. What MapInk stores on your device

MapInk stores the map projects you create locally in the app's sandboxed storage on your iPad. This can include:

- **Drawn map strokes** and their colors.
- **Pins** you add, including titles, notes, coordinates, and route order.
- **Apple Pencil notes** saved as map-anchored strokes.
- **Saved projects** containing your strokes, pins, and notes.
- **App preferences** such as selected map style, drawing style, and interaction state.

Delete a project in the app and that saved project is removed from local storage. Delete MapInk and the app's local data is removed from the device. If you use iCloud device backups, local app data may be included in backups according to your Apple device settings; those backups are managed by Apple, not MC Software.

## 3. Location

MapInk may ask for Location permission so it can center the map near your current area. Location is optional: you can still use the app by searching for places or panning the map manually.

MapInk does not send your location to MC Software. Map display, place search, and directions are provided by Apple's MapKit services, and Apple's privacy practices apply to those services.

## 4. Apple MapKit services

MapInk uses Apple's MapKit services for:

- Displaying standard, hybrid, and satellite maps.
- Searching for places and addresses.
- Calculating driving and walking routes.
- Showing route distance and estimated travel time.

These services are operated by Apple. MC Software does not receive your map searches, directions requests, or location data from Apple.

## 5. Sharing

If you choose to share an annotated map snapshot, MapInk creates an image from your current map project and opens the standard iPadOS share sheet. You control where that image goes next.

## 6. What we do not do

- We do not operate a server for MapInk.
- We do not create user accounts.
- We do not collect analytics or telemetry.
- We do not include advertising or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.
- We do not receive your saved projects, pins, notes, or sketches.

## 7. Children's privacy

MapInk is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, or from anyone else, through MapInk.

## 8. Your rights

Under data protection laws including the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal data. Because MC Software does not receive or store MapInk app data, there is typically no MapInk personal data held by MC Software for us to provide, correct, or delete. Data stored locally by the app is under your control on your device.
