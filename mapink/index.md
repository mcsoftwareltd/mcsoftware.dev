<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">What it does</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">A map canvas for iPad.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <p>MapInk turns Apple Maps into a visual planning surface. Search for a place, drop pins, sketch paths, write Apple Pencil notes, and keep everything attached to the map.</p>
      <p>Use it for trip ideas, field notes, route sketches, meeting points, lessons, or any plan that makes more sense when you can draw over real places.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">Feature highlights</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">Pins, routes, ink, and projects.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Draw on the map</strong> — sketch freehand lines and see useful distance labels.</li>
        <li><strong>Add places</strong> — search places and addresses, then save them as titled pins with notes.</li>
        <li><strong>Plan routes</strong> — calculate driving or walking trips through your saved pins.</li>
        <li><strong>Write with Apple Pencil</strong> — save handwritten notes that stay anchored to map locations.</li>
        <li><strong>Save projects</strong> — keep strokes, pins, and Pencil notes together, then reopen them later.</li>
        <li><strong>Share snapshots</strong> — export a clean image of your annotated map.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">Privacy</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">Your map projects stay local.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <p>MapInk has no account system, no MC Software backend, and no advertising or tracking SDKs. Your saved projects live on your iPad in the app's local storage.</p>
      <p>Maps, place search, and route calculation are provided by Apple MapKit. Location access is optional and used to center the map near you.</p>
      <p>Read the <a href="{{ '/mapink/privacy/' | relative_url }}">full privacy policy</a>.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">Requirements</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">What you need.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <ul>
        <li>iPad running iPadOS 17.4 or later.</li>
        <li>Apple Pencil support is recommended for handwritten notes, but not required for pins, search, and route planning.</li>
        <li>Internet access is required for maps, place search, and route calculation.</li>
        <li>Location permission is optional; you can search or pan the map instead.</li>
      </ul>
