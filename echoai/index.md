<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">What it does</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">Record, transcribe, understand.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <p>Start a recording before a meeting, a lecture, or a conversation. EchoAI transcribes it in real time, keeping up with multiple speakers and noisy rooms. When you stop, it writes a concise summary, pulls out action items, and files everything into your session library.</p>
      <p>Every session is searchable. Ask EchoAI a question — <em>"What did we decide about the launch date?"</em> — and it finds the answer across all your recordings using on-device semantic search.</p>

<section class="product-section">

    <p class="product-section__label">How your data stays yours</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">Everything runs on your iPhone.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <p>EchoAI uses Apple's on-device Speech framework and WhisperKit to turn audio into text. Summaries and action items are generated by <strong>Foundation Models</strong> — Apple Intelligence's on-device language model. Speaker separation runs locally through SpeakerKit.</p>
      <p>No audio, no transcripts, and no summaries ever leave your device. There is no MC Software backend. EchoAI has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no account system, and no tracking of any kind.</p>
      <p>You can read the <a href="{{ '/echoai/privacy/' | relative_url }}">full privacy policy</a> — it's short, because there isn't much to say.</p>
