<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">What it does</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">Plan the route before the engine starts.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <p>RideNav is built for riders who care which road they take. Drop waypoints, reorder stops, close the loop back to where you started, and switch between <strong>fast</strong>, <strong>balanced</strong>, and <strong>twisty</strong> route preferences without leaving the map.</p>
      <p>When the plan is set, you can record the ride live, watch the route follow you, and export the result as a GPX file for your archive — or import one in if your favourite road already lives in another tool.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">Group rides</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">Keep the group together in realtime.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <p>Start or join a group ride and RideNav publishes your live position to every other rider, and shows their pins on the same map. Regroup spots, fuel stops, and route changes update for everyone as they happen.</p>
      <p>A built-in messenger-style chat sits over the map for quick rider alerts without leaving the ride view. Friends and family who aren't riding can follow along through a shared live tracking link.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="product-section">
  <div class="container container--narrow">
    <p class="product-section__label">Feature highlights</p>
    <h2 class="product-section__title">Built for long rides, bad signal, and the unexpected.</h2>
    <div class="product-section__body">
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Offline maps</strong> — download areas before you leave and keep the ride usable when coverage drops.</li>
        <li><strong>Weather context</strong> — hourly forecasts and road-relevant conditions while you plan.</li>
        <li><strong>Hazard reporting</strong> — drop a pin on a hazard, get a proximity alert when you approach one.</li>
        <li><strong>GPX import &amp; export</strong> — bring planned routes in, take recorded rides out.</li>
        <li><strong>Web planner</strong> — sketch the route on a big screen before you set off, with your account synced to the bike.</li>
        <li><strong>Glove-friendly controls</strong> — high-contrast route lines and large hit targets that stay readable at speed.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
